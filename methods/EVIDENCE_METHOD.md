# Evidence Method

## Core Rule

> Public artifacts may compress the evidence. They may not elevate it.

The public story is never the source of truth. It is a controlled derivative
of qualified private evidence.

## Evidence Chain

```text
repository event
-> immutable source
-> atomic fact
-> bounded claim
-> internal qualification
-> public-safe transformation
-> public evidence identifier
-> case narrative
```

## What the Private Evidence Authority Records

For each of the 25 indexed engineering-event claims in this candidate, the
private evidence layer records:

- the exact historical source;
- the supported fact;
- the evidence class;
- the environment in which the event occurred;
- prohibited stronger conclusions;
- the strongest supported claim;
- known limitations;
- review and disclosure state.

The 25 engineering-event claims displayed in this candidate each have a
public identifier and a private derivation entry recording their reviewed
source claims, evidence class, execution setting, and required limitation.

That method establishes a controlled evidence trail. It does not establish
third-party validation, independently reproduce the underlying events, or
establish production performance or market value.

## What the Public Layer Receives

The public layer receives only:

- a plain-English statement;
- a sanitized public evidence identifier;
- the evidence class;
- a generalized environment;
- the important limitation;
- a machine-readable public-safe receipt candidate.

It does not receive private source paths, commit identities, raw receipts,
private control implementation, private control topology, bypass-relevant
details, prompts, credentials, or private architecture.

## Evidence Classes Used Here

### Live-context evidence

The behavior ran against a real local gate, runtime, deployment, or provider
interface.

This does not automatically mean production, customer-facing operation, or
external validation.

### Repeated local evidence

Focused tests or controlled local executions reproduced the behavior.

This is not a production reliability benchmark.

### Working artifact

An implemented software or documentation artifact exists and received
bounded verification.

## Review Independence

Internal review checked source fidelity, market language, and claim
boundaries. For the 58-case deep-dive review, the preserved record reports
review against recorded artifact hashes but does not independently prove
reviewer-role separation. No internal review is represented as third-party
validation.

## Supersession

If later evidence contradicts a public statement:

1. the statement is placed on hold;
2. dependent case language is reviewed;
3. the public derivative is repaired or withdrawn;
4. the stronger wording does not survive merely because it was already
   published.

## Market Evidence

Current state: `NONE`

Technical evidence can support an application today. Market evidence must be
earned later through external review, pilot use, adoption, payment, or
measured outcomes.
