# Authorship and AI Use

## Required Disclosure

> Tavio Lawrence defined the objectives, system architecture, authority
> boundaries, constraints, acceptance gates, evidence requirements, claim
> limits, and integration decisions. AI agents performed bounded
> implementation, analysis, drafting, and review work under those controls.
> Internal agent review is not represented as independent third-party
> validation.

## What Human-Controlled AI Assistance Means Here

AI agents contributed to:

- bounded implementation;
- test and receipt analysis;
- source-to-claim mapping;
- drafting;
- internal review;
- revision after identified defects.

The human role included:

- defining the intended outcome;
- selecting the architecture;
- defining permissions and prohibitions;
- approving phase boundaries;
- deciding what counted as acceptable evidence;
- setting claim ceilings;
- deciding whether work could advance.

## Why This Disclosure Matters

The portfolio does not hide AI assistance or present generated work as
unaided.

It demonstrates a deliberate engineering method in which AI agents can
contribute substantial work without becoming the source of their own
authority, evaluation standard, or final claims.

## Review Boundary

Internal AI review occurred within the same project. The public evidence does
not independently prove reviewer separation, and no internal review is
presented as external validation.
