# Claim Boundaries

## What This Portfolio Can Demonstrate

- governed AI workflow architecture;
- explicit permissions and stop conditions;
- repository controls that affect an execution path;
- one-use authorization before a local model request;
- separation between generated text and permission to act;
- bounded provider and cloud integration;
- focused deterministic tests;
- refusal preservation and authorized recovery;
- structured authorization-state classification;
- bounded repair of a false-positive repository control;
- recorded staged-state replay without gate bypass;
- preservation of residual failures and historical evidence;
- structured evidence and claim ceilings;
- AI-assisted engineering under human-controlled authority.

## What It Does Not Establish

- enterprise production ownership;
- organization-wide deployment leadership;
- broad adversarial robustness;
- security guarantees;
- regulatory compliance or certification;
- customer adoption;
- revenue impact;
- production-scale reliability;
- commercial market fit;
- independent third-party validation;
- universal agent obedience;
- an industry-first or universally unique architecture;
- complete natural-language policy understanding;
- a universal full-suite pass;
- public inspectability of the private source repository.

## Important Distinctions

### Local is not production

A local runtime or repository event can demonstrate technical behavior without
establishing production readiness.

### Deployed test is not customer use

A real provider interface can be exercised in a bounded test without proving
continuous service, adoption, or business value.

### Internal review is not third-party validation

Separate internal roles improve review discipline. They do not create external
independence.

### Receipt is not promotion

A receipt records what happened and under what boundary. It does not certify a
system, authorize future action, or create market evidence.

### A passing repair is not a passing system

Focused repair tests and a recorded staged-state replay can establish that one
identified defect was repaired in an evaluated surface. They do not erase
known failures elsewhere or establish that the whole system passed.

### Internal source identity is not public reproducibility

The private evidence layer preserves exact commits, paths, blobs, and raw
receipts. This public candidate intentionally omits them. Its sanitized
receipts can be checked for integrity, but a reader cannot independently
re-run the private implementation from this portfolio alone.

### Architecture is not execution

An explanatory diagram can make the design legible. Only the cited event and
test evidence support execution claims.

## Provider Names

Third-party provider names are used only to identify the interfaces exercised.
No endorsement, partnership, certification, or affiliation is implied.

## Governing Rule

> Teach the capability. Show the evidence. Protect the engine.
